# Zurich City Tour – Topic List (Peter)

Stand 7 November 2026 · Tour ID `zurich-city-tour` · Language: English

## How to use this list

Every Zurich City Tour POI script is tagged against these topic IDs, so the app's told-once registry knows what the user has already heard.

- **Introduces:** topics the script explains in full (FULL version) when they haven't been told yet. A CALLBACK version is required where the topic appears at more than one POI.
- **Calls back:** topics the script only references in 1–2 sentences, and only if they've already been told.
- **What counts as a topic:** something that comes up at two or more POIs, or that the listener needs to understand several stops. Single-POI facts stay in that POI's script.
- **Flags:** **V** = verify before recording (dates or current status that can change). **S** = sensitive. Per your input, no topic carries an S flag; see the baseline rules at the end.
- **IDs are stable.** Don't renumber; add new ones at the end of a category.

## Baseline rules (no S flags)

You chose to treat no topic as sensitive. I kept only these minimal rules, because they protect the guide and the app rather than restrict content. Delete them if you disagree.

1. **Facts stated plainly**, including dark ones (Manz's execution, Waldmann's execution). No jokes about victims.
2. **In the Q&A, Peter gives no personal opinion on current votes, parties or politicians.** He can explain what is being debated.
3. **Current status claims** (banks, companies, events) carry an "as of" caveat in the Q&A.
