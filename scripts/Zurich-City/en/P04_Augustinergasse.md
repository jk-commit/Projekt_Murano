## 🔊 ANFAHRT

Next up is a short, colourful lane full of painted wooden bay windows sticking out over the street. Walk into it and look up.

## 📌 STANDORT-CHECK (nur wenn P03 auch im Anfahrts-Radius ist)

You're in the Ow-goos-tee-ner-gah-seh, under the painted bay windows. The church with the big clock is a separate stop.

## 📍 KERN

Welcome to the Ow-goos-tee-ner-gah-seh, probably the prettiest lane in Zurich. Look at those painted bay windows, the oriels. They weren't just decoration. They let more light into narrow houses, and they were perfect for watching who was walking down the street. Medieval doorbell cameras, basically.

Craftsmen lived and worked here: carpenters, painters, printers. The lane is named after the monastery of the Augustinian monks at the end of it.

[[REF-01 CALLBACK]]
After Zwingli's Reformation, the monks were gone. Zurich turned their church into a workshop and a mint. From prayers to coins: about as Zurich as it gets.
[[/REF-01]]

As an art lover, I like that every oriel is different. Some are painted with flowers, some with coats of arms, some in colours that would never get through a planning office today. And as a cook, I always wonder what was going on in the kitchens behind them. Probably a lot of cabbage.

One tip: the big shopping street, Bahn-hohf-shtrah-seh, is just at the end of this lane. Same city, completely different price list.
