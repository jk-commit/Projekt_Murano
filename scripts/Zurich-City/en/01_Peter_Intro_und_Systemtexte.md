## 🎙 MEET PETER — guide intro (~25 s)

*For the tour's start page.*

Hi, I'm Peter! I grew up in Basel, which makes me a professional rival of everyone in Zurich. Then I moved here, fell in love with the city, and spent years cooking in a Michelin-starred kitchen. Now I show people around instead of plating their dinner. Today I'll take you through two thousand years of Zurich: Romans, reformers, bankers, artists, and the best things to eat on the way. Don't tell anyone in Basel I said that.

*Plays after tour start when the user is inside the tour area. Deliberately names no first stop, because the route is dynamic.*

Grew-etsi, and welcome to Zurich! That's how you say hello here. You're standing in the old town, right where the river Limmat leaves the lake. On both banks, the city grew from a Roman customs post into one of the richest places on earth.

Here's how this works: the map shows you the next stop. I'll chat to you on the way, and when we arrive, just stop for a moment, and I'll start. Got a question? Tap the microphone and ask me. Ready? Let's go. The first stop is only a few minutes away.

## 🎙 OUTRO (~30 s)

*Plays after the last POI of the generated route.*

And that's our tour! Romans, rebel reformers, women in armour, bankers, Dada and Lenin, all in a few hundred metres. Thanks for walking with me.

My tip to finish: for a drink with a bit of art, go to the bar of the Kronenhalle, near the opera house. There are real paintings on the walls, by artists you've heard of. Order something local, look around, and toast to Zurich. Even a guy from Basel will do that. Ciao!

## System texts

### S-NAH — two stops close together (~10 s)

Quick heads-up: two stops are right next to each other here. Check the map, stand in front of the right one, and stay still for a moment. Then I'll start.

### S-STEHEN — waiting for the user to stop (~5 s)

We're here. Stop for a moment, and I'll tell you the story.

### S-UNTERBRECHUNG — user walks away mid-story (~6 s)

I'll pause here. When you're back in front of it, I'll carry on.

### S-ABSCHWEIFEN — user leaves the route (~12 s)

*Trigger: more than about 150 m from the planned corridor for more than about 2 minutes. Then the pop-up "Pause tour" / "Continue tour".*

Hey, I notice you're wandering off on your own. Great, that's how you really get to know a city! Just let me know when you're ready, and we'll carry on.

### S-PAUSE — user taps "Pause" (~5 s)

No problem, we're on pause. The map stays open. Tap "Continue" whenever you like.

### S-WEITER — user taps "Continue" (~6 s)

Welcome back! The map shows you the next stop you haven't seen yet.

### S-SICHER — short safety line (~5 s)

*Once per tour, at P11 Niederdorf. Zurich is very safe; this is a light reminder for crowded evenings.* (V: confirm with city police advice before recording.)

Zurich is very safe, but in busy crowds, keep your phone and bag close.

*Once per tour.*

By the way: if you want to know more about anything, just tap the microphone and ask me.
