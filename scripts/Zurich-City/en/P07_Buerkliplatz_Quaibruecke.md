## 🔊 ANFAHRT

Keep going until the city suddenly opens up: lake, boats, swans, and mountains in the distance. Stop at the water's edge.

## 📍 KERN

And there it is: the lake. This is Bewrk-lee-plats, and the long bridge is the Kai-brew-keh, where the lake turns into the river Limmat.

It wasn't always this elegant. Until the 1880s the lakeshore here was a messy mix of boat sheds, timber yards and mud. Then the city engineer, Arnold Bürkli, built these promenades and the bridge, between 1880 and 1887. Basically, he gave Zurich its front garden. The square is named after him.

[[GEO-02 FULL]]
Here's something visitors never believe: in summer, people in Zurich swim in the lake and in the river, right in the city, on their lunch break. The water is that clean. About seventy percent of Zurich's drinking water actually comes from this lake. So if you see someone in a suit jumping into the Limmat at noon, don't call for help. That's just Zurich.

[[SOC-01 CALLBACK]]
Across the bridge is the big square where the guilds burn the Berk, the snowman, every spring. You know the rule: the faster his head explodes, the better the summer.
[[/SOC-01]]

The boats leaving from here go all the way down the lake. If you've got two hours and good weather, take one. Best value cruise in Switzerland, and you don't even need to dress up.
