## 🔊 ANFAHRT

Ahead is a busy square with trams going in every direction, and a little red cable car disappearing up the hill. Head for the cable car station.

## 📍 KERN

This busy square is called Tsen-trahl, named after the Hotel Central built here in 1883. And that cute red cable car going up the hill is the Polly-bahn, running since 1889. It takes students up to the university in a couple of minutes. Lazy? No: efficient. Very Swiss.

[[C19-02 CALLBACK]]
The main station is just across the river. Zurich's railway boom, the one that also gave us the Bahnhofstrasse, is why this whole area grew so fast.
[[/C19-02]]

[[C19-03 FULL]]
Up there is the ETH, the Federal Institute of Technology, founded in 1855. It's one of the best universities in the world, and a certain young man called Albert Einstein studied there. Legend says he wasn't the most disciplined student. I find that very comforting.
[[C19-03 CALLBACK]]
Up the hill is the ETH, the university where Einstein studied.
[[/C19-03]]

[[ECO-01 CALLBACK]]
And who owns this little cable car? A bank, of course: UBS. Even the funicular in Zurich has a banker.
[[/ECO-01]]

Take it up if you can. At the top there's a terrace with one of the best views over the old town. You've just walked through all of it. From up there, you'll see your whole tour at once.
