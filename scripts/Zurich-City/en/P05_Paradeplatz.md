## 🔊 ANFAHRT

Now you're coming to a big square full of blue trams, grand stone buildings and serious-looking people in suits. Careful with the trams, they don't wait.

## 📍 KERN

Welcome to Pa-rah-deh-plats, the money heart of Switzerland. Fun fact: this square used to be a pig market. Today it's where the big banks have their headquarters. Some people say not much has changed. I couldn't possibly comment.

[[ECO-01 FULL]]
Zurich's rise as a banking city really took off in the 19th century. In 1856 Alfred Escher, the railway pioneer, founded the bank that became Credit Suisse, and it later made this square its home. For over 160 years it was one of the two Swiss giants, until it ran into trouble and was taken over by its rival UBS in 2023. Two banks, one square, and now one name.
[[ECO-01 CALLBACK]]
The banks you've heard about are all around you: this square is where Swiss money has its address.
[[/ECO-01]]

[[C19-02 FULL]]
The street running through here is the Bahn-hohf-shtrah-seh. It was built from 1864 on top of an old moat, the Frog Moat. Yes, the most expensive shopping street in Switzerland was once a ditch full of frogs.
[[C19-02 CALLBACK]]
And the Bahn-hohf-shtrah-seh you might know already: once a frog-filled moat, now a very expensive street.
[[/C19-02]]

Now my favourite building here, as a chef: Shprewng-lee, the chocolate and pastry shop. It moved onto this square in 1859, because the family hoped the main railway station would be built here. It wasn't. They stayed anyway, and they were right. Try a Luxemburger-lee, a tiny, light macaroon. Buy a box. You won't regret it. Your waistline might.
