## 📍 KERN

Welcome to the Linden-hohf, my favourite balcony in Zurich. Down there is the Limmat, across the river the old town climbs up the hill, and on a clear day you can see all the way to the Alps.

[[ROM-01 FULL]]
This is where Zurich began. There was a Celtic settlement up here first, and from around 15 BC the Romans. They ran a customs post on this hill: everyone moving goods towards the Alps paid a toll. We even know the town's Roman name, Too-ree-kum, thanks to one single gravestone for a little boy, found up here in 1747. His father was the local customs officer. So Zurich's very first written name comes from a tax collector. Some things never change.
[[ROM-01 CALLBACK]]
Up here stood the Roman customs post I told you about. Too-ree-kum, a town of toll collectors, on a hill with a very good view of everyone who owed them money.
[[/ROM-01]]

Now look at the fountain with the woman in armour. In 1292, so the legend goes, the Habsburg army stood outside the city while most of Zurich's men were away. The women put on helmets and armour, marched up here, and from a distance looked like a whole new army. The Habsburgs packed up and left. True or not, I love that the city's favourite war story is a costume party.
