## 🔊 ANFAHRT

Follow the cobbled pedestrian street full of little shops, bars and restaurants. Stop on the small square with the fountain.

## 📍 KERN

Welcome to the Nee-der-dorf, the "lower village". In the Middle Ages this was the poorer, rougher end of town. Today it's where Zurich goes out: bars, little restaurants, music spilling out of doorways. During the day it's charming. At night, it's loud. I recommend both.

[[ARC-01 CALLBACK]]
Look up: more of those painted bay windows you've seen, perfect for keeping an eye on the street.
[[/ARC-01]]

[[SOC-03 FULL]]
Now, as a former chef, let me tell you what to eat in Zurich. The classic is Zurich-style sliced veal in a creamy mushroom sauce, with Rösti, crispy grated potatoes. Rich, simple, perfect after a long walk. I worked for years in a Michelin-starred kitchen here, and I can tell you: the best version is often in a simple old guild restaurant, not a fancy one. Don't tell my old boss.
[[SOC-03 CALLBACK]]
And if you're hungry: this is a good street for that classic Zurich veal with Rösti I mentioned.
[[/SOC-03]]

For drinks, this is the right neighbourhood. Wander the side lanes rather than the main street: the smaller the bar, the better the stories. Try a local beer, or a glass of white wine from the vineyards around the lake. Yes, Zurich makes wine. Basel people don't like to admit it.
