## 🔊 ANFAHRT

Look up for a church tower with an enormous golden clock face. You'll find it on a small, quiet square that feels like a village.

## 📌 STANDORT-CHECK (nur wenn P04 auch im Anfahrts-Radius ist)

You're on the little square in front of Saint Peter. The lane with the painted bay windows round the corner is a separate stop.

## 📍 KERN

This is Saint Peter, Zurich's oldest parish church. Now look up at that clock. The face is eight point seven metres across, the largest church clock face in Europe. For a long time it set the official time for the whole city. If your watch disagreed with Saint Peter, your watch was wrong. Very Swiss.

The tower had a second job too: until 1911 a fire watch lived up there, looking out over the roofs day and night.

[[MED-03 FULL]]
And inside this church lies the man who changed how Zurich was governed: Rudolf Brun. In 1336 he teamed up with the city's craftsmen, threw out the old council of nobles and merchants, and set up a new order built on the guilds. He became Zurich's first mayor, and then made sure nobody could remove him. For centuries afterwards, if you wanted a say in this city, you joined a guild.
